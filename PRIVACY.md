# Privacy Policy for Undock Engine

**Last Updated**: September 2026

**Undock Engine** is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Undock Engine does **not** collect, store, track, or transmit any personal data, email content, browsing history, or user activity. All processing happens entirely within your local web browser session.

## 2. No External Network Requests
The extension makes **no** network requests to any third-party servers, analytics services, or external APIs. 

## 3. Permissions Used & Purpose
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to store your preferred window dimensions (width, height) and screen coordinates locally so that the detached reading window re-opens in the same position on your multi-monitor setup.
- **`host_permissions` (`https://mail.google.com/*`)**: Required solely to read the DOM structure of Gmail's reading pane, project it into a local secondary window, and delegate triage shortcut actions (such as archive or star) back to your active tab.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing & Commercial Sale
We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your information to any third parties under any circumstances.

## 5. Contact & Open Source
Undock Engine is open source. You can inspect the source code and report any questions or issues directly on GitHub:
https://github.com/undock-engine/undock/issues
